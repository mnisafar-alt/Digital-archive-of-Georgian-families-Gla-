The Gogoshvili Family

Gogoshvili (გოგოშვილი) is a Georgian surname documented in Georgian historical and genealogical materials.

The Georgian form is გოგოშვილი, while the common Latin transliteration is Gogoshvili.

Name and Origin

The surname Gogoshvili consists of the root “Gogo” and the Georgian suffix “shvili” (შვილი).

The suffix “shvili” expresses descent or child/offspring in Georgian surnames.

The precise origin of the root and whether all Gogoshvili families descend from one common ancestor requires further historical and genealogical research.

Historical Distribution

Georgian genealogical data records the Gogoshvili surname in several settlements of Kakheti, including Bodbe, Dedoplistsqaro, Vardisubani, Telavi, Kardanakhi and Sighnaghi.
