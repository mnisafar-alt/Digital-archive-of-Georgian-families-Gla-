The Onikashvili Family

Onikashvili (ონიკაშვილი) is a Georgian surname documented in Georgian genealogical material relating to the 18th century.

The surname Onikashvili appears in a list of Georgian surnames associated with the 1721 census. This provides evidence that the surname was already in historical Georgian records in the early 18th century.

Origins

The currently available sources do not provide sufficient evidence to identify one specific progenitor or to establish a single noble origin for all families bearing the Onikashvili surname.

Therefore, the precise origin and genealogy of the surname should be considered a subject for further historical, archival and genealogical research.

Historical Record

One of the important early records of the surname is its appearance in material associated with the 1721 census. Georgian genealogical sources list Onikashvili among the surnames recorded in the 18th century.

Modern Distribution in Georgia

Contemporary surname data confirms the continued presence of Onikashvili in Georgia. Important areas of concentration include Tbilisi, Kakheti and Shida Kartli.

Onikashvili Families in Fereydan

The Onikashvili surname is also found among Georgian communities in Fereydan, Iran.

A published report about Erekle Onikashvili states that his ancestors originated from Kakheti and that he himself was born in Fereydan.

This information is relevant to research into the migration history of Fereydani Georgians, but it should not automatically be generalized to every Onikashvili family.
