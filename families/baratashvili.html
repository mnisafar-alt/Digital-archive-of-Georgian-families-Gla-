The Baratashvili Family

The Baratashvili were one of the important noble and feudal families in the history of Georgia. They became known under the name Baratashvili from the end of the 15th century and remained significant in Georgian political and social life until the 19th century.

Origins

According to the Georgian Encyclopedia, the progenitor of the lineage was Barata Kachibaidze (Kachibadze). His children, who lived during the second half of the 15th century, initially continued to bear the Kachibadze surname. Their descendants became known as Baratashvili from the end of the 15th century.

Sabaratiano

The historical estate of the Baratashvili was known as Sabaratiano. It mainly included the Algeti, Ktsia and Mashavera valleys in Kartli.

Samshvilde and Other Centers

Samshvilde was an important center of the Baratashvili estate. The family also possessed fortresses, palaces and estates in Dmanisi, Darbaschala, Tbisi, Enageti and other locations.

Political and Military Role

During the 16th and 17th centuries, the Baratashvili were among the leading noble families of the Kingdom of Kartli and played an important role in its political and military affairs.

Religious Centers

Important religious centers associated with the family included the monasteries of Pitareti and Gudarekhi, Dmanisi Sioni Church and the church of Kedi village.

Branches

During the 16th and 17th centuries, several branches emerged from the Baratashvili lineage and developed into separate feudal families.

  • Gostashabishvili
  • Germanოზishvili
  • Zurabishvili
  • Abashishvili
  • Qaplanishvili
  • Palavan-Khosroshvili
  • Iaralishvili

Notable Members

Notable members of the family included Barata Baratashvili, Mikheil Baratashvili, Nikoloz Baratashvili and Sulkhan Baratashvili.

Nikoloz Baratashvili (1817–1845) was one of the most important poets in Georgian literature.
